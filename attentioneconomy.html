Project Case Study: Attention Economy

The Goal:

During my time at CART, I was given a 3 day assignment to craft a full essay regarding the Attention Economy. This is a special type of economy that social media platforms engage with to try and get people to come to their app and stay for longer and longer.

Concepts Learned

  • Essay Synthesization: I learned how to synthesize data from multiple, online and offline sources in my paper.
  • Arguing the middle ground: In my essay, I didn't particularly argue in favor of one side over the other, instead, i argued that the situation was too complicated to be put on such a simple scale.

Challenges & Solutions

  • The Problem: I wasn’t sure of the effects of the attention economy.
  • The Solution: I researched through my chosen sources to find how the attention economy affects everyone’s day to day lives, and how it affects the economy as a whole. I also researched the different sides of the argument to find out how to argue the middle ground.
  • The Problem: I had to integrate a poem from the 1600’s into my essay.
  • The Solution: I implemented the poem in a way that draws a connection between the attention economy of then, and now, showing how the attention economy has been around for a long time, and how it has evolved over time.
  • The Problem: We had only 3 days to complete this project, and all of those days we could only work on it during class.
  • The Solution: I used my skills to correctly identify parts of the essay that need more time put into them, and parts that can be done quickly, to ensure that I was able to complete the project on time, while still putting in the necessary amount of work into each part.

Habits of Mind in Action:

  • Investigate: This project required extensive research throughout the entire process to find sources, fact check statements, and proofread.
  • Initiate: Throughout this paper, I had to make many decisions about what to spend the most time on, taking initiative to develop the best paper I could in the short amount of time I was given
