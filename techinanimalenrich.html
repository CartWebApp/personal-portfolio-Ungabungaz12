Project Case Study: Technology in Animal Enrichment

The Goal:

While working with the Fresno Chaffee Zoo, I was assigned a research project to create a paper for the use of technology in Animal Enrichment. This Paper went into detail over how technology can assist zoo specialists in their fields better than previous methods.

Concepts Learned

  • Essay Construction: I emboldened my knowledge of how to construct a perfectly formatted MLA essay, including how to structure paragraphs’ information for easier reading flow.
  • Technology Use: Through my research, I was able to learn about the different ways technology is used in animal enrichment, some examples being sound systems, scent systems, and lighting systems.

Challenges & Solutions

  • The Problem: I wasn’t sure how to format my essay at first.
  • The Solution: I researched the different methods and standards before landing on MLA, an acceptable standard used all the way from grade school to college.
  • The Problem: I needed sources for the paper, to both prove the paper right and to integrate California State Standards for English to synthesize sources
  • The Solution: I researched extensively, finding documents, essays, test results, and other similar papers to cite and use on my own.
  • The Problem: There wasn’t much usable research about specifically technology in zoo scale animal enrichment.
  • The Solution: I broadened my scope to include papers about the effects animals have under certain conditions that can be replicated by technology.

Habits of Mind in Action:

  • Investigate: This project required extensive research throughout the entire process to find sources, fact check statements, and proofread.
  • Persist: This paper was our English half-year final, we started it a lot earlier than that, so we had to persist through, even when papers that took us a while to read ended up not helping our paper at all.
