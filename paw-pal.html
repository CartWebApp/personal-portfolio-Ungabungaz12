Project Case Study: Paw-Pal

The Goal:

Paw-Pal was a mock pet wellness tracker web app, where, at my time at the Center for Advanced Research and Technology (CART), I worked on with a partner.

The Technology Stack:

  • Languages: HTML5 and CSS3
  • Tools: Figma for extensive user interface development options.
  • Deployment: Hosted via Netlify to ensure global accessibility and performance.

Concepts Learned

  • Design Process: I strengthened my knowledge of Figma’s design tools to create both low-fidelity and high fidelity designs with my partner.
  • References: Throughout the process I had continuously referenced online resources such as W3Schools and the MDN Web Documentation

Challenges & Solutions

  • The Problem: Within the Figma design, I didn't know how to allow for multiple pet profiles in a more unique way to stand out against the competition.
  • The Solution: I did some research into methods of user design and landed on a carousel design for the pet profile selector that would allow for selecting pets with a simple swipe and tap.
  • The Problem: Our CSS design solutions weren’t optimized for editability later on, if we decided.
  • The Solution: We used tools and games such as CSS Zombies and CSS Grid Garden to strengthen our CSS styling skills for this project.
  • The Problem: We had differing ideas on how the website should look for different pages.
  • The Solution: Me and my partner worked together to develop a development plan where we each would develop one half of the website.

Habits of Mind in Action:

  • Persistence: We faced many errors during the development process, including almost losing half of it due to merge conflicts. But we pulled through.
  • Communication: Throughout the process, we communicated with each other to develop the site with our combined visions.
